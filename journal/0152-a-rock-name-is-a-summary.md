## The question that would not stay small

P-1 — the identity question of the stratigraphic-correlation design — asked what
fills the ground between a siltstone borehole and a mudstone borehole: literal
mixtures (M-A), an octaves cut into interfingering bodies (M-B), or a split by
material relation (M-C). M-C's cost was "a relation predicate on the registry," and
the user asked the right prior question: **how do we currently know what materials
are continua — bootstrap artifact, or ratified relationship?**

The sweep answer was clean: **nothing declares continua today, and the thing that
looks like it does is barred from the job.** Class co-membership (mudstone and
siltstone share `clastic-fine`) is a 2026-07-18 *selection* contract — formation
windows and abundance — whose second members are literally annotated `(roster
proof)` in `geology.rs`; and P11's A-CLEAN ruling had already barred any class view
from storage and physics. Meanwhile the honest ingredients existed elsewhere,
all ratified within the week: U1's grain-is-an-AXIS, U4's φ ladder, FS-A's
literature-cited release spectra, and `settle_energy`'s derive-don't-table
precedent. The relation M-C needs could be **computed** from data we already
believe, rather than authored as a new hand taxonomy.

## The widening, and why it was the right order

The user then asked the question under the question: what IS a material for this
sim — and are siltstone, mudstone, granite even species? The petrology is
unambiguous and it is the design argument:

- **Clastic rock names are texture labels on a conserved mineral stock.** The
  Wentworth fences define mudstone/siltstone/sandstone by grain-size fraction of
  whatever minerals arrived; mechanical weathering re-sorts that stock without
  changing it. Sandstone → sand is one substance changing state, and the same
  quartz grain survives many such cycles.
- **Igneous names are regions on continuous composition diagrams** (QAPF), and
  granite-vs-rhyolite is a *texture* axis at fixed composition. Even plagioclase
  is a solid-solution continuum. Names are fences on continua most of the way down.
- **Identity genuinely changes only at chemical edges** — feldspar to clay,
  calcite into solution. Mechanical is re-sorting; chemical is transformation.
- **Coal is the roster's confession:** compressed dead plants in a world with no
  plants. The seams are Biofacies proxies wearing a rock's name — the user's own
  framing, and the reason the roster question was live at all: *"i'm not married
  to a single material in the roster yet."*

## The ruling

**Materials are pack-authored labels over regions of a continuous term space**
(`materials.md` § DECIDED 2026-08-04; the user: *"that's it. I'm onboard"*). The
record stores the name; expression moves within regions and blends between them;
the space between names is mixture. Relations are derived from declared axes —
never hand-paired. Members that cannot write an honest term definition are
PROXIES, marked with heirs. The capability (term schemas, per-term blend and
conservation, the mixture channel, term-keyed kernels) is engine; every axis,
label, relation, and the philosophy itself is pack — decided in each future case
by the subtraction test from earlier in the same session: subtract the pack and
what remains must be featureless and conservation-true.

This is S-3 said in petrology: a rock name is a *summary* of composition and
state, and the summary derives from the authority. The loose forms made this move
first (U1); the lithified roster now follows the same symmetry without a rebuild —
the record keeps its 8 bytes, and every name simply owes a definition that could,
in principle, re-derive it.

**P-1 resolved into the ruling as M-C-with-derived-predicate** (banner on the
correlation audit): continuum iff regions adjoin on a declared axis → mixture;
discrete → the octaves cut; sharp only where a process declares a break. The § 3.3
tension between the two user principles resolved rather than picked a side —
smoothness governs continua, dress-every-contact governs the cut.
