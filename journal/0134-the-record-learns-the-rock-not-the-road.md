# 0134 — the record learns the rock, not the road

*2026-08-01, the "geo" session — the day a clarifying question about storage shapes
became the largest architectural ruling since the north star.*

The session resumed the fluvial member's record-terms design pass under the new
one-topic-per-message format the bodies thread had just instituted. The first two
rulings landed cleanly: the load's **composition** gets recorded (the per-species split
the transport solve computes and discards — a recording change), while the **grain-size
continuum** was sequenced as its own modelling arc (P10) rather than faked from
`grain_size_mm`, whose three measured defects — a collision, an inversion, no gravel —
made the proxy indefensible. Mobility died its literature death (a chapter is ~62.5 Myr;
the slowest published channel migration sweeps a valley thousands of times per chapter,
so only *confinement* is recordable at 460 m), and χ was specced but deferred behind the
unbuilt WINDOW axis — with a closed-aggregator-vocabulary sketch filed as that future
pass's input.

Then the user asked how a unit could store only an argmax when a real bed is a mixture —
and the answer ("the layer's identity is genuinely lossy; expression re-diversifies
within the winner's family") surfaced the deeper defect. Their next observation cut the
knot the corpus had been circling for a week: *"we'll just keep re-deriving and punting
this... **members need to go into deep history, proper.**"* Class-grade deep time and
transitions-on-materials (DECIDED 2026-07-22) were on a collision course — a sim that
only knows "clastic-fine" structurally cannot consult mudstone's declared edges — and
every prior encounter with the wall had been survived by punting.

The design pass ran the same day and the rulings came fast, two of them off the user's
own blade:

- **The record names `MaterialId`** — because once fitness runs at deposition time, the
  member has done its whole job. The fitness envelope is *selection machinery*; history
  records **the rock, not the road to it**.
- **Representation A-CLEAN, no surviving class view** — option D (a blessed derived
  class view for the fixed-N storage sites) died on the user's one-line challenge:
  *"are they just places the old shape is entrenched?"* Checked honestly: yes, all of
  them. The far `ShareVec<6>` serves rejected-interim semantics; the transport planes
  need per-cell support, not a global roster; and `LithoResistance` was per-material all
  along. The physics never needed classes — only the indexing did.
- **Sparse day one** for the transport planes — dense scales as cells × registry size,
  the wrong asymptote for a plugin-first engine with an open material registry.
- **U5 dissolved, not ruled** (corrections #84): "can a pack member move terrain?" had
  been answered on 2026-07-19 by the user's own world-identity rule (*"patch set joins
  pack set in world identity"*) — a different pack set IS a different world — and P11's
  record-bake kills the one real hazard (retroactive change to an existing world's
  ground). The integrator presented it as a live three-option decision anyway; the
  user's memory caught it within one message. Second instance of #81's shape in three
  days: **grep for the prior ruling before relaying a NEEDS-RATIFICATION item.**
- **MM-3 folds in** — one surgery over the shared files, landing the near-path
  restructure on the record it will actually serve.
